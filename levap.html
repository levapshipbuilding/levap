What we do

SIX DISCIPLINES.
ONE CREW.

Executed with the precision applied on the world's biggest ships.

01

Piping Installation & Maintenance

Complex systems, clean installs. Full piping scopes from routing to pressure testing — with crews who've done it on the world's biggest ships.

02

Steel & Welding Works

Structural and outfitting steel, certified welding to classification society standards. When the weld has to hold, we're who you call.

03

Engine Maintenance

Routine overhauls to emergency repairs. Our engine teams work fast under pressure and always to spec. No guessing. No delays.

04

HVAC Installation & Maintenance

Climate systems for vessels where downtime isn't an option. Installed right the first time, maintained to keep them that way.

05

Interior Installation & Maintenance

From crew quarters to passenger areas — joinery, panels, flooring. We turn raw steel into spaces people want to be in.

06

Manning & Workforce Solutions

Skilled hands on short notice. Pre-vetted welders, fitters and pipe engineers — ready to integrate with your team from day one.
